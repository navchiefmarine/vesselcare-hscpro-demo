Applicability Method Important

Controls are not assumed to apply merely because the craft operates in Indian waters. The final vessel configuration should consider craft type, GT, flag, passenger/industrial-personnel status, certification basis, voyage/operating area, construction date, machinery, persons carried, Class notation and any DG Shipping/MMD/RO directions applicable to the specific craft.
